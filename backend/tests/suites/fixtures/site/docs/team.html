Team

Inviting a team member

To invite a team member open Team and choose Invite. Enter their email address and pick a role. The invite email expires after seven days.

Roles and permissions

Owners manage billing and team members. Admins manage invoices and clients. Viewers can only read. You can change a member role at any time from the Team page.

Removing a member

Remove a team member from the Team page. Their invoices stay in the account.
