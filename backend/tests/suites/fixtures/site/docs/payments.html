Payments

Payment methods

Clients can pay an invoice by card or by bank transfer. Card payments settle in two working days.

Refunds

To refund a payment open the paid invoice, choose Refund and enter the amount. Partial refunds are supported.

Failed payments

When a card payment fails the invoice stays unpaid and the client is asked to try another card.
