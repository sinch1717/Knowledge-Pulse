Invoices

Creating an invoice

To create an invoice open Invoices and choose New invoice. Pick the client, add line items with a quantity and a price, and set the due date. The invoice number is assigned automatically.

Editing an invoice after sending

You can edit an invoice after sending it. Open the sent invoice and choose Edit. Kestrel keeps the original version and emails the client the corrected invoice with a note that the amount changed.

Sending invoices by email

Send an invoice by email from the invoice page. The client receives a link to view and pay the invoice online.

Invoice reminders

Automatic reminders email the client three days before the due date and again when the invoice is overdue. Turn reminders off per invoice or per client.
